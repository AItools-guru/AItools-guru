# Day 5. Text. Meeting bot compliance

## Post body (paste this into LinkedIn)

Your VP of Legal has no idea what your meeting bot stores.

Every meeting gets transcribed. That transcript hits a server. It sits there. Indefinitely.

Names. Salaries. Hiring opinions. Legal exposure. Health details someone mentioned in passing.

All of it. Indexed. Searchable. Discoverable in litigation.

Most meeting tools store transcripts by default. They call it a feature. Your compliance team calls it a liability.

I built Kall AI with one rule that is not negotiable: store nothing.

Audio comes in over TLS 1.3. The model processes it. Structured backlog comes out. Then the data is wiped.

No transcript database. No recordings sitting on a server for three years. No compliance officer losing sleep.

100% ephemeral. Zero retention.

Enterprise teams run planning meetings where someone says "we should fire the contractor" or "the client lied about scope." That sentence lives on most platforms forever.

On Kall AI, it does not exist 10 seconds after processing.

Your meeting tool should not double as a liability archive.
