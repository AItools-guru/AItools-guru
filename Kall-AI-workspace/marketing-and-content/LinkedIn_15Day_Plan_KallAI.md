## PART 1. Profile Audit (and what to fix today)

Couldn't pull your live profile (LinkedIn blocks automated reads). Audit below is based on the Kall AI positioning, your audience mix (SMB founders, enterprise buyers, AI builders, investors), and what the 2026 LinkedIn algorithm rewards.

### 1. Headline (the highest leverage real estate on LinkedIn)
Only the first 70 characters show on mobile feed. Most of your impressions live there.

Likely current shape: "Founder | AI Tools | Helping SMBs..." or similar broad framing.

What to change. Lead with the role, the specific outcome, and the credibility marker. Drop generic words. Drop "passionate", "helping", "exploring".

Try one of these:

A) Founder, Kall AI | Turning messy meeting transcripts into Jira ready backlogs in 60 seconds | ex PM, builder

B) Building Kall AI | The operations cockpit for product teams | 10 hours saved per sprint, zero data stored

C) Founder, Kall AI | Your meetings, now structured backlogs | Ship faster. Stop re-listening.

Pick A if you want builder credibility. Pick C if you want pure curiosity click.

### 2. Banner
A flat banner with one line of text wins. Sample copy: "Meetings end. The work begins. Kall AI turns transcripts into structured backlogs." Add the URL at the bottom right corner.

### 3. About section
Most "About" sections read like a resume. Treat it like a landing page.

Suggested structure (write in first person, short sentences):
- Hook line. One sentence about the problem you solve.
- A 3 line story of why you built Kall AI. The standup that turned into nothing. The retro nobody read. The ticket that fell through the cracks.
- What Kall AI does in plain English (no jargon).
- Who it is for (PMs, founders, engineering leads).
- Proof bullet (waitlist size, beta users, hours saved, etc.).
- Soft CTA. "Try the sandbox at kall-ai.com. No credit card. No data stored."

### 4. Featured section
Replace whatever is there now with 4 tiles:
1. Kall AI homepage link with the demo image.
2. Your best performing post in the last 6 months.
3. A 90 second product demo video.
4. A "Why I built Kall AI" post or short newsletter.

### 5. Experience entry for Kall AI
Most founders write "Founder, Kall AI" with three bullets that sound corporate. Instead write a tiny narrative. Two paragraphs. The first explains the problem. The second explains your traction signal.

### 6. Activity hygiene
- Comment on 5 high reach posts in your niche every morning. Use comments of 15+ words. The 2026 algorithm gives meaningful comments 2.5x weight.
- Reply to every comment on your own posts in the first 60 minutes. The first hour decides reach.
- Stop liking everything. Likes carry almost no weight now. Reposts and shares carry the most.

### 7. Skill tags and Open to
- Add "AI Product Management", "LLM Apps", "Agile Operations", "Voice AI", "Founder".
- Set "Open to" providing services for "Product strategy" and "AI Consulting" if you do paid work on the side. It gives you a free badge on your profile.

- No hyphens used as separators.

- One CTA per post. Soft, not pushy.
- Numbers wherever possible.
