# Day 7. Text post. 30 day reflection.

## Post body (paste this into LinkedIn)

30 days into building Kall AI.
Here is what I got wrong, in order.

I thought the hardest part would be the AI.
It was not. The hardest part was deciding what to leave out.

I thought users wanted features.
They wanted speed. They wanted trust. They wanted the thing to not break.

I thought I would code most of it.
I shipped most of it by writing crisp specs and letting the model do the heavy lifting.

I thought I would launch when it was perfect.
I launched when it was useful. There is a difference.

I thought a clever name would carry me.
It did not. Demos did.

30 days in. The thing is alive. The thing is being used. People are sending me messages at 1 AM telling me their sprint planning got 40 minutes shorter.

That is enough for now.

If you are 30 days into anything that scares you, keep going.
