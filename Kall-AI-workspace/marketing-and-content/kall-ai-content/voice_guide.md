# Voice Guide for kall-ai-content

## The single most important rule
Sound like a human typing on a phone. Not a brand. Not an AI. Not a copywriter.

## Sentence rules
- Average 8 to 14 words.
- Vary length. Mix a 4 word line with a 16 word one.
- Start sentences with verbs more often than nouns.
- One idea per sentence.

## Punctuation rules
- No em dashes. None. Anywhere. Replace with a period or a comma.
- No en dashes either.
- No hyphens used as sentence separators. (Compound words like "follow up" stay as two words. "Build in public" stays as three words.)
- Periods, commas, question marks, and line breaks only.
- Ellipses are allowed but rarely.

## Paragraph rules
- 1 to 2 lines per paragraph.
- White space wins. The eye needs rest.
- Long blocks of text die on the LinkedIn feed.

## Hook patterns that work
Pick one of these patterns for the first 2 lines. Never start with "In today's world", "As a founder", "I want to share", "Excited to announce".

1. Cost line. "I lost a $40K deal because of one missed action item."
2. Confession. "I almost quit on Kall AI three weeks in."
3. Specific number. "12 seconds. That is how long it took to turn a 45 minute standup into a backlog."
4. Hot take. "Whisper is overrated for product teams."
5. Question that hurts. "How many hours per week does your team waste in meetings?"
6. Persona open. "Meet Priya. Ops lead at a 14 person agency."
7. Anti pattern. "Stop writing meeting notes."
8. Contradiction. "The next billion dollar productivity tool will not be a notes app."

## CTA patterns that work
Pick one per post. Never two.

1. Soft sandbox. "Try the sandbox at kall-ai.com. No card. No data stored."
2. Real question. "What is the most expensive thing you ever forgot from a meeting?"
3. DM nudge. "DMs open."
4. Save trigger (carousels). "Save this before your next sprint planning."
5. Build in public ask. "Follow me here. I am posting the rest of the build."

## Word choices
- Use "we" when Kall AI has a team. Use "I" for solo builder posts. Pick per post. Do not switch inside one post.
- Use "you" liberally to put the reader in the post.
- "build in public" not "building in public" when used as a noun phrase.
- "kall-ai.com" lowercase always.

## Hashtag rules
- Exactly 3 per post. Never more.
- One broad (#AI, #ProductManagement).
- One niche (#PydanticAI, #VoiceAI, #AgileTools).
- One audience (#Founders, #SmallBusiness, #Engineering).
- No hashtags inside the body. Always at the end.

## Things that kill reach in 2026
- Outbound links in the body of the post. (Always drop the link in the first comment if you want max reach. The CTA URL inside the body is OK once but never twice.)
- Tagging more than 2 people.
- Engagement bait. ("Like if you agree.")
- Generic emojis at the start of every line.
- Asking "thoughts?" as a closing line.

## Things that lift reach in 2026
- Posts that hold readers for 31+ seconds (dwell time is king).
- Carousels (PDFs hit 6.6% average engagement).
- Comments of 15+ words on your post in the first hour.
- Replying to every comment in the first hour.
- A controversial line that invites disagreement (not insults).
- A real, specific number.
- A real, specific persona.

## Sanity check before you ship
Before writing the file, run this checklist mentally:
1. Did I read kall_ai_facts.md and pull at least one real fact?
2. Are there zero em dashes in the post?
3. Are there zero hyphens used as sentence separators in body copy?
4. Is the hook strong enough to beat the "see more" cutoff?
5. Is there exactly one CTA?
6. Are there exactly 3 hashtags?
7. Is there a seed comment of 20+ words ready to post?
8. Does it sound like a human typed it on a phone? (Read it aloud.)

If any answer is no, rewrite.
