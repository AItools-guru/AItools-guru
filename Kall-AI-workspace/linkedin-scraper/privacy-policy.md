# Privacy Policy for Kall AI LinkedIn Analytics Exporter

*Last Updated: June 28, 2026*

This Privacy Policy describes how the **Kall AI LinkedIn Analytics Exporter** Chrome Extension ("the Extension") collects, uses, and shares information.

## 1. Data Collection & Processing
The Extension operates **entirely local to your browser**. 
*   **LinkedIn Analytics Data**: The Extension parses your LinkedIn Recent Activity feed to extract post content, impressions, reactions, comments, and reposts. This data is processed in volatile browser memory and immediately converted into a downloadable CSV file.
*   **No Server Ingestion**: We do not collect, store, or transmit your LinkedIn data to any external servers.
*   **No Analytics or Tracking**: We do not inject tracking cookies, analytics pixels, or any telemetry scripts.

## 2. Permissions & Data Use
The Extension requires the following browser permissions to operate:
*   `activeTab` / `scripting`: Needed to inject the export button and run the local scraper script on your active LinkedIn activity page.
*   `downloads`: Needed to trigger the native browser download prompt to save the CSV file on your local machine.
*   `host_permissions` (`https://www.linkedin.com/in/*/recent-activity*`): Scopes the extension's execution strictly to your personal LinkedIn Recent Activity page.

## 3. Data Protection
Because all data processing occurs on your local device, your data is protected by your local system security. The Extension does not store any persistent cookies or cached files on your browser.

## 4. Compliance with Google Policies
The Extension complies with the Google Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
